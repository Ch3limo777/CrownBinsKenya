2. Billing System Requirements
2.1 Objectives
Replace the current manual/Zoho One + spreadsheet process with an integrated, automated billing system that tracks payments, generates invoices and receipts, and keeps records updated in real time — without manual re-entry after each payment.
2.2 Who Uses the System
Role
Description
Customer
Registers/logs in, views bills, pays, downloads invoices & receipts, raises complaints, receives notifications
Admin
Day-to-day operations: manage customers, invoices, payments, complaints, collection schedules
Super Admin / System Administrator
Full system control — everything Admin can do, plus manage users & roles, permissions, system settings, and add/remove Admins
2.3 Pricing Model
Pricing is not fixed — each bag type (Green, Blue, Black) has a price range rather than one set price.
Bag types and pricing are the same across residential (individual & apartment) and commercial customers.
Customers choose their bag colour/type (Green, Blue, or Black) based on quality and price preference.
Bag quantity is tied to collection frequency: once-per-week collection = 4 bags/month; twice-per-week collection = customer chooses between 8, 10, or 12 bags/month.
Actual price per customer is negotiated directly with CrownBins staff.
Customers requesting a quotation are directed via a WhatsApp button/link on the website to negotiate pricing with staff — the system does not auto-calculate quotes.
2.4 Payment Subscription Plans
Billing is subscription-based, not tied to a fixed short cycle — customers choose to pay every 3 months (quarterly), every 4 months, or every 6 months (semi-annually).
The subscription interval determines when the customer's invoice is generated and becomes due.

M-Pesa (Paybill and Till) integration is required for automatic payment confirmation. Bank transfer is not limited to one bank — customers may pay from any bank, so the system should allow entry of the paying bank's name/reference rather than assuming Equity. Bank transfer, cheque, and cash payments are recorded and marked by staff.
2.6 Payment Reconciliation — Matching Payments to the Correct Customer
A payment made outside the customer's portal (e.g., paid directly via M-Pesa Paybill or Till using a phone number not registered in the system) does not automatically identify which customer it belongs to. This needs a clear process:
“Pay Now” on the portal opens a payment method selection screen, showing all supported options (Safaricom Paybill, Safaricom Till, Bank Transfer, Cheque, Cash) so the customer chooses how they want to pay, rather than defaulting to one method.
Preferred method — STK Push from the portal: if the customer selects M-Pesa (Paybill or Till), the system triggers an M-Pesa prompt tied to their invoice. The person completing the prompt can use any phone number (their own, a family member's, anyone paying on the customer's behalf) — the payment is still correctly linked to that customer's invoice, since it originated from their account.
If the customer selects Bank Transfer, Cheque, or Cash, the portal displays CrownBins' payment details (bank account info, or instructions to pay at the office / to a collection agent) and the invoice remains Pending until staff confirm and mark it as paid.
Fallback — direct Paybill/Till payments made outside the portal: the system first attempts to auto-match the payment using the paying phone number against registered customer numbers.
If no match is found, the payment is flagged as “Unmatched” and placed in a review queue for Admin.
Admin manually links the unmatched payment to the correct customer account by searching name, amount, date, or M-Pesa transaction code.
Customers can also submit their M-Pesa transaction code via the portal or WhatsApp after paying directly, to speed up matching by staff.
Once matched (automatically or manually), the invoice status updates to Paid and the receipt is generated and sent as normal.
2.7 Invoicing
Billing cycle: invoices generated automatically according to the customer's chosen subscription plan (every 3, 4, or 6 months) — no fixed 2-week cycle.
Invoices marked Paid automatically once a matching payment is confirmed (never marked paid in advance).
Invoice format: PDF, in addition to SMS / WhatsApp / email delivery of the invoice.
Invoice fields: invoice number, customer name, date, bag type & quantity, amount, payment method, transaction ID/M-Pesa code (for Paybill/Till/bank payments), company logo and details.

The client's current invoicing (via Zoho One, shown in reference screenshots) uses a familiar, proven layout. The recommendation is to keep that overall structure and workflow — which staff are already used to — while tailoring the specific fields and options to how CrownBins actually operates and to the Kenyan market. This should be confirmed with the client before final build:
Kept from the Zoho-style layout:
Customer selector that auto-fills contact details
Auto-generated invoice number, invoice date, and due date
Line-item table with quantity, rate, and auto-calculated amount/total
Notes / terms field
Save as Draft → Save & Send workflow
Tailored for CrownBins & the Kenyan market:
Currency shown as KES throughout
TBD — Whether VAT and/or KRA PIN fields are required, particularly for commercial/business customers
Line items pre-populated from the customer's service plan (bag type — Green/Blue/Black — quantity, and subscription period) rather than free-text generic items
Payment method field limited to CrownBins' actual methods: Safaricom Paybill (with account number), Safaricom Till Number, Bank Transfer (any bank), Cheque, Cash — with a transaction ID/M-Pesa code field for Paybill, Till, and bank payments
Delivery built in as first-class options: SMS, WhatsApp, and email (not just email, as in generic Zoho templates)
CrownBins branding: logo, off-white / deep charcoal / organic green palette, tagline — replacing the generic template look
2.9 Receipts
Receipt generated and sent automatically immediately after a payment is confirmed.
Delivered via SMS, WhatsApp, or email — supporting the customer's preferred channel(s), which may be one or both.
Format: PDF, plus the messaging/email text itself.
Customers can view and download past receipts and invoices anytime from their account, to their preferred channel (WhatsApp or email).
2.10 Reminders & Overdue Bills
First reminder sent 1 day after the due date.
Reminders repeat every 2 days until the bill is paid or manually marked as paid.
No penalties for late payment — reminders only.
2.11 Overpayments, Mistaken Payments & Refunds
System accepts overpayments or payments made in error.
System automatically notifies the customer of the overpayment/discrepancy.
Resolution (refund, credit, correction) is handled through the Complaints feature or direct staff support — not automated.
2.12 Notifications
Payment received confirmations
Upcoming/overdue payment reminders
Collection reminders (e.g., “your garbage will be collected tomorrow”)
Service updates and general announcements
Delivered via SMS, WhatsApp, and/or email based on customer preference
2.13 Complaints
Customers can raise complaints (e.g., missed collection, damaged bin, billing issue) from their account.
Admin/Super Admin can track complaint status (e.g., In Progress, Assigned, Resolved, Pending) as shown in the reference dashboards.
2.14 Data Migration
Existing customer and billing records currently held in Zoho One and spreadsheets will need to be exported and imported into the new system. Zoho One supports data export (CSV/API), which should make this a straightforward migration step once field mapping is confirmed.
